# Per-paper notes

Use `papers/<short>.md` in the topic folder. Use the method name, or first author and year, as the short name. Check title or DOI/arXiv id before writing; update an existing note and preserve the user's comments. Use author/year citations in prose.

```markdown
---
title: "<full title>"
簡稱: "<method or author/year>"
年份: <year>
出處: "<venue and version>"
用途: <核心／支撐／背景／待讀／未分類>
狀態: <已讀全文／已讀相關段落／部分閱讀／待讀>
連結: "<paper URL or DOI>"
程式碼: "<confirmed URL or search status>"
讀取日期: <date>
---

# <short name>

**閱讀紀錄**：<text coverage; viewed figures/total and pages; tables; cropped equations>
**論文群中的角色**：<what this paper explains about the main paper>

## 問題與方法
<failure/limitation and source; design and reason; input → operations → output>

## 圖表與證據
| 圖表／主張 | 條件與結果 | 原文位置 | 支持範圍 |
|---|---|---|---|

## 改善與限制
**作者明列的局限**：<brief quotation and location>
**我的判讀**：<labelled interpretations and unresolved questions>

## 知識點與用途
<knowledge-point ids/links; how this paper contributes to the cluster>
```

Keep scope, figures and evidence traceable; do not repeat the full teaching page in notes. A short recap may record the problem, supported improvement and remaining question.
